title: "Autodesk Fusion 360"
layout: post
---

Autodesk Fusion 360 is a cloud-based 3D CAD, CAM, and CAE tool that was released after Autodesk Inventor. It offers a more affordable pricing plan and even provides a free license for hobbyists, making it accessible to a wider audience. Fusion 360 is known for its intuitive interface, which makes it particularly suitable for beginners. However, users may experience some lag due to its cloud-based nature.

I used Fusion 360 mostly for my hobby projects, which including interior making and 3D-printing. Although the compenents management concept of Fusion 360 is excellent, it demands too much processing power and is not suitable for a big, complicated project.
