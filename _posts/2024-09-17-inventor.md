Autodesk Inventor is a professional 3D CAD software for product design and engineering. It offers parametric tools for creating precise models, detailed drawings, and simulations, helping to improve product quality and reduce costs.

I chose Inventor as my first 3D parametric design because of its relationship with the Autodesk family, thus make my learning curve much more gradual. I did not use all the tools that Inventor provided, only poly modeling and assembly tools were used for my projects with robots.
