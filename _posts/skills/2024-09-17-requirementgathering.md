Requirement gathering is one of the fundamental skills I acquired during my IT Business Analysis Mentorship program. This crucial process involves collecting, documenting, and managing the needs and expectations of stakeholders for a project or system. Here are some key aspects of requirement gathering that I learned:

1. Elicitation Techniques:
   - Interviews
   - Surveys and questionnaires
   - Workshops and focus groups
   - Observation
   - Document analysis

2. Types of Requirements:
   - Functional requirements
   - Non-functional requirements
   - Business requirements
   - User requirements
   - System requirements

3. Documentation:
   - Use cases
   - User stories
   - Requirements traceability matrix
   - Business process models

4. Validation and Verification:
   - Stakeholder reviews
   - Prototyping
   - Requirements prioritization

5. Tools:
   - JIRA for requirement management
   - Confluence for documentation
   - Balsamiq for wireframing
   - Lucidchart for process modeling

Mastering requirement gathering has been essential in my journey to become an effective Business Analyst, as it forms the foundation for successful project outcomes and stakeholder satisfaction.
