Okay, "good" is a strong word. But I am confident to say that I am good at learning new things. I don't want to say I am good at anything specifically, because Aristotle once wrote: 

**“The more you know, the more you realize you don’t know”**

Thing is, you don't need to know everything, as the pareto principle stated that you only need 20% of your knowledge to solve 80% of your problems. The rest 80% of the knowledge you need only in case of 20% of the problems. That is why I wanted to be a generalist, and this specific blog is a playground, a demonstration of what I am capable of, what I could contribute, and what I desire. You can read about the inspiration about this blog in the previous post here: [Hello World]({% post_url 2024-09-19-hello-world %}).
